### OpenStreetMap and offline search

The POI loader already refreshes Waterloo-area OpenStreetMap data into Postgres. Its bbox (`43.40,-80.67,43.56,-80.42`, south/west/north/east) includes parts of Kitchener so nearby services across the city line can be search destinations. Its fields include the OSM object type and ID, `name`, classification tags (`amenity`, `shop`, `leisure`), node coordinates, and way or relation geometry. The loader covers grocery stores, schools, colleges and universities, parks, libraries, community centres, pharmacies, and clinics. Geofabrik's Ontario `osm.pbf` is the same upstream data in a file suitable for offline extraction and routing. OpenStreetMap data is ODbL 1.0. Keep the attribution and licence with any distributed extract. [OpenStreetMap licence](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) · [Geofabrik Ontario extract](https://download.geofabrik.de/north-america/canada/ontario.html)

The Overpass services tested timed out or rejected the full bbox query. Small OpenStreetMap API extracts succeeded for 11 of 20 tiles, yielding 59 distinct objects that match the loader's category tags, 37 with names. This is a partial source check. A count for the whole bbox and a comparison of resolved destinations against independently verified places are still pending. [OpenStreetMap map endpoint documentation](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/API_v0.6#Retrieving_map_data:_GET_/api/0.6/map)

For a fast search path, use a locally refreshed catalogue so query-time resolution can run without Overpass. Use City POIs and the library directory to cross-check coverage. Reconcile City and OSM entries for the same physical place while keeping distinct branches, campuses, and entrances selectable. Counts of source features alone cannot establish the search accuracy target in the spec. [Waterloo Public Library locations](https://www.wpl.ca/your-library/locations-hours/)

## Census and rent benchmarks

The Waterloo census loader reads the fixed 2021 snapshot in `db/seed/waterloo-census-2021.json`. Run `pnpm data:civic areas` before `pnpm data:civic census`. The snapshot contains 155 distinct DA centroids and 764 distinct block points on surface, calculated in EPSG:3347 from the cartographic boundaries and stored as WGS84 longitude/latitude. The loader assigns each point to one district using the current city polygons. The source observations retain Statistics Canada flags 2 and 6 for non-numeric values. DA characteristics are 229 (median income), 1400 (tenure total), 1402 (renter count), 1476 (tenant count), and 1478 (tenant shelter-cost burden).

The district block totals reconcile to 121,436 residents, 52,049 private dwellings, and 47,040 occupied private dwellings. Published DA renter counts sum to 18,130, 75 below the independently published city total of 18,205. The loader preserves the published counts and reports shares only where all assigned DA tenure observations are available. Multi-DA shelter-cost figures are labelled as estimates and weighted by published tenant counts. The exact positive-income tenant denominator is unavailable in the DA profile. District rates with missing observations for tenant households stay unavailable. [Archived Statistics Canada Geographic Attribute File](https://data.source.coop/dataforcanada/d4c-datapkg-statistical/archive/statcan/geographic_attribute_file/2021_92-151_X.zip) · [Archived Statistics Canada Dissemination Geographies Relationship File](https://data.source.coop/dataforcanada/d4c-datapkg-statistical/archive/statcan/dissemination_geographies_relationship_file/2021_98260004.zip)

- **2021 Census Profile (`98-401-X2021006`).** Filter to dissemination areas (DAs) in the City of Waterloo census subdivision `3530016`. The live SDMX queries supply geography DGUID (`REF_AREA`), `CHARACTERISTIC`, `STATISTIC`, `GENDER`, `TIME_PERIOD`, `OBS_VALUE`, and flags. Characteristic `1` is population, `1400` is the tenure total and `1402` counts renter households. `1476` counts tenant households in non-farm, non-reserve private dwellings. `1478` reports the share spending at least 30% of income on shelter. The shelter-cost-to-income measure applies to households with positive income, so verify a matching tenant denominator before weighting DA rates. In one inspected DA, `1402` reported 15 renter households, `1476` reported zero, and `1478` was suppressed. Preserve missing values and flags, and allow for random rounding when checking neighbourhood sums. The City profile confirmed 18,205 renter households (38.7% of 47,040) and a 40.9% tenant shelter-cost burden share. The City's separate district PDF covers 18 planning districts and can cross-check tenure figures where boundaries and definitions align. ## Police data and neighbourhood rates

The 2025 Waterloo Regional Police Service occurrence workbook downloaded and parsed successfully. It has 292,876 occurrence rows and 23 columns. `Municipality=WAT` identifies 29,829 rows and 125,940 rows region-wide lack a municipality code. All `WAT` rows carry projected X/Y text in `GeographicLocation`, across 1,475 distinct points, and 29,697 have `NearestIntersectionLocation`. Other fields include the occurrence number, patrol division and zone, reported and dispatch timestamps, initial and final call types and descriptions, priorities, and disposition. The data dictionary describes coordinates normalized to nearby intersections and some placeholder locations. [2025 XLSX download page](https://wrps.ca/resource/2025-occurrence-data) · [WRPS data dictionary](https://www.wrps.on.ca/en/resourcesGeneral/Occurrence-Data/WRPS---About-our-data---2021---AODA-copy.pdf)

The workbook measures calls for service. Common records include vehicle stops, property-damage collisions, proactive initiatives, and bylaw complaints. A call count divided by neighbourhood population would measure calls, with some boundary misallocation from location normalization. WRPS makes the data available for public research but provides no explicit reuse licence on the publication pages. Statistics Canada's official police-reported crime rates are available for the Kitchener–Cambridge–Waterloo CMA. Those rates cannot be assigned to individual City neighbourhoods. For #4 / #21, keep neighbourhood safety scoring off with these sources. [WRPS statistics and data](https://wrps.ca/front-page/statistics-and-data) · [Statistics Canada CMA crime rates](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/en/daily-quotidien/260722/t004a-eng.htm)

## Utility-cost source check

For #8, omit the utility estimate from monthly costs for now. Waterloo Region sets these allowances for its subsidized housing program. Listings still report whether utilities are included when the source states it.

Toronto's source is a schedule of affordable-housing rent deductions for tenant-paid hydro, heat, water, and hot water, by unit size. Waterloo Region does publish official utility adjustments for rent-geared-to-income housing. Its housing-provider page links the 2020 guide, whose appendices list monthly electric- and gas-heating deductions (for example, $47 and $32 for a two-bedroom apartment) and separate charges when the housing provider supplies some utilities. Both publishers' schedules are administrative figures. The check found no public Waterloo schedule covering the same four utilities on Toronto's basis, and no official Waterloo private-market utility bill estimates by unit size. City water tariffs and affordable rent caps do not provide that breakdown.
